Help & Instructions

Test structure

The test has two sections. Each section is split into two modules, and each module is timed separately. You cannot return to a module once its time is up.

  • Reading and Writing: 2 modules, 32 minutes each
  • Break: 10 minutes between sections
  • Math: 2 modules, 35 minutes each; a calculator is allowed on every question

Answering questions

  • Click an answer choice to select it. Click a different choice to change it.
  • Use Mark for Review to flag a question to come back to.
  • Turn on ABC (cross-out) to strike through choices you've ruled out.
  • Open Question _ of _ at the bottom to jump to any question or the review page.
  • Math grid-in questions: type an integer or decimal. Include a minus sign for negatives.

Tools

  • Directions — reopen the instructions for the current module.
  • Timer / Hide — hide the countdown if it's distracting.
  • Calculator and Reference — available during Math modules.

Scoring

After the last module you'll get an estimated score on the 400–1600 scale, a per-module breakdown, and a review of every question with the correct answer and a short explanation. These estimates are approximate and meant for practice.
